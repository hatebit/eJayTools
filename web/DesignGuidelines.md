
# eJay Brand Design Guidelines

## 1. Brand Personality

The eJay visual identity is built around a bold, energetic, modern, and playful aesthetic.

The brand should communicate:

- **Energetic:** Dynamic and attention-grabbing.
- **Modern:** Contemporary and bold.
- **Playful:** Friendly and approachable.
- **Confident:** Strong and recognizable.
- **Creative:** Expressive with movement and distinctive typography.

## 2. Color Palette

The primary color palette consists of orange, black, and white.

| Color | Hex Code | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Orange | `#F58220` | Main brand color, highlights, accents |
| Deep Black | `#111111` | Typography, outlines, contrast |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Backgrounds and negative space |

## 3. Typography

### Primary Typography
- Bold and extra-bold sans-serif fonts.
- Rounded and expressive letterforms.
- Slightly italic or forward-leaning styles.
- Strong visual presence.

### Secondary Typography
- Clean, modern sans-serif fonts.
- Simple and readable letterforms.
- Regular or medium font weights.

## 4. Logo Design Principles

The eJay logo has four defining characteristics:

1. **Bold lettering:** Thick, rounded characters.
2. **Layered outlines:** Black borders surrounding orange lettering.
3. **Dynamic movement:** Forward-leaning typography suggesting speed and energy.
4. **Signature swoosh:** Curved orange underline creating a distinctive finish.

### Logo Usage Rules

- Preserve the original logo proportions.
- Do not stretch, compress, or distort the logo.
- Maintain the original color combination.
- Maintain sufficient clear space around the logo.
- Avoid visually busy backgrounds.
- Do not add unnecessary effects or outlines.

## 5. Graphic Elements

The visual language should incorporate:

- Curved shapes and flowing lines.
- Dynamic swooshes and motion-inspired elements.
- Bold orange and black contrasts.
- Expressive typography.
- Clean layouts with sufficient white space.

## 6. Layout and Composition

- Use strong visual hierarchy.
- Keep the logo prominent.
- Use orange to highlight important information.
- Incorporate curved graphic elements as accents.
- Maintain consistent spacing and alignment.
- Avoid overcrowding designs.

## 7. Brand Applications

The visual identity can be applied across:

- Business stationery.
- Social media graphics.
- Marketing and promotional materials.
- Posters and advertisements.
- Branded merchandise.
- Digital and web materials.

All applications should maintain consistent typography, color, and graphic styling.

## 8. Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use orange as the main accent | Introduce too many colors |
| Use bold typography | Use weak, thin lettering |
| Preserve logo proportions | Stretch or distort the logo |
| Use dynamic curves | Overuse decorative elements |
| Maintain good contrast | Use low-contrast combinations |
| Keep layouts clean | Overcrowd compositions |

## 9. Overall Design Direction

**BOLD. DYNAMIC. DISTINCTIVE.**

Every eJay design should remain:

- Bold and recognizable.
- Energetic and expressive.
- Consistent with the orange-and-black identity.
- Balanced between strong graphics and clean layouts.
